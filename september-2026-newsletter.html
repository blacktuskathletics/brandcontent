Subject line: When the smoke rolled in, you came inside anyway
Preview text: A thank-you from the BBQ, a send-off for Coach Eve, and the launch of Base Camp for runners.

Hey team,

August did a strange thing to this city. For a good stretch of the month the sky went the colour of a nickel, the wildfire smoke rolled in, and air quality warnings had half of Edmonton checking the index before they laced up. If you're a runner, you know the feeling: the outdoor plan you had gets quietly cancelled by the air itself. And here's what we noticed from the floor. You came inside anyway. The room stayed full on the smokiest days, and there's something fitting about that, because the whole idea we're built on is that the work doesn't stop when the conditions turn. It just moves indoors. Here's a look back at August, and the big thing we're kicking off in September.

Coming up: the next few weeks

The season is turning, and for runners that's not a loss. It's the starting gun.

All SeptemberBase Camp launches: strength for runners

This is the big one. September we launch Base Camp, our off-season strength program built for runners. Here's the idea in one line: you can't build real strength and peak mileage at the same time, so the off-season is your one window to build the engine instead of just running it into the ground. Stronger legs mean a faster stride and far fewer of the injuries that flare up every spring. Whether you've got one day a week to hold your ground or two to build something new, we mapped the whole season out. Want the free guide? The equinox makes it official: the off-season window is open. For a runner, that's not a sad thing. It's the moment the work that makes next spring your best one actually begins. No better time to start than now.

All monthStructure beats willpower

Fall is when routines either set or scatter. The trick isn't more discipline, it's structure: a class time already on the calendar, a coach expecting you, a plan you made when you were motivated that keeps running when you're not. Anchor it now while the energy's high. StrongTEENS (11-16) and our Legends (50+) crew are both great places to lock a fall rhythm as school heads back.

Want a little extra support?

If you have questions about your training, your goals, or building an off-season that fits your running, we're here for you. Sometimes a quick chat is all it takes. Book a No Sweat Intro at blacktuskathletics.com, DM us "BASE CAMP" for the free runner's guide, or just reply and tell us what you want this fall to look like. We read every one.
