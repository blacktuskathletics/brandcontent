Subject line: A cool July, and one August date to circle
Preview text: New gear on the floor, a heartfelt send-off for Coach Eve, and the Summer BBQ on Aug 22.

Hey team,

Let's be honest about July: it stayed on the cool side. No real scorcher, a few degrees below the usual most of the month, and the city spent its evenings at K-Days and Taste of Edmonton instead of the beach. You showed up anyway. Rain or shine or a mild grey Tuesday, the floor stayed full, and that is the whole point of this place. Here's a look back at the month, and one August date you'll want to circle.

Last month at Black Tusk

A cool, full month, built around the people.

Jul 16-26The city came out to play

K-Days and Taste of Edmonton pulled the whole city out for ten days straight, and you proved again that you can enjoy every bit of it and still keep your training alive. Patios and midway one night, back on the floor the next. That balance is the skill nobody talks about, and you've got it.

New faces at Bring-a-Friend

[A great turnout / X guests] came through for Bring-a-Friend, and a few of those faces are already back. That's exactly the point. Thank you to everyone who brought someone; introducing a friend to this room is one of the kindest things you can do.

On the floorNew gear landed

We added some things we've been wanting for a while: fresh kettlebells, a set of smaller dumbbells (the jump from the 10s to the 15s is a real thing), and a couple of trap bars for deadlift variations that are friendlier on your back and let you move some serious weight. If you haven't put your hands on them yet, grab a coach and we'll show you where they fit.

Wins worth a shout

A shout to [member(s)] for [milestone / PR], and a warm welcome to [our newest members]. The room felt it, and so did we.

A heartfelt send-off for Coach Eve

Thank you, Eve.

This one comes with a lot of love. Coach Eve is stepping back from her coaching role with us [after (date)]. Anyone who's trained with Eve knows what she brought to the floor: the care, the attention, the way she made people feel like they belonged here from day one. That kind of coaching leaves a mark, and hers is all over this community.

We're going to miss seeing her in her usual spot, and we're grateful for every rep, every cue, and every encouraging word she gave this gym. Please join us in thanking Eve for everything. [Stay tuned for a proper send-off.]

Coming up: the next few weeks

Enjoy the city, gather the community, then build the rhythm that carries you into fall.

Sat Aug 22The Black Tusk Summer BBQ

Circle it now. On Aug 22 the gym spills out [into the lot / to the park] for food, family, and the people who make this place what it is. Bring your crew, bring a friend who's been curious about us. [Time and full details to come.] This is the one you don't want to miss.

Aug 1-3Heritage long weekend

Happy Heritage Day from all of us. The Heritage Festival is on at Borden Park. Get out, eat your way around the world, and we'll see you on the floor when you're ready.

Aug 6-9Folk Fest on the hill

The city heads to Gallagher Park for the Edmonton Folk Music Festival. Go enjoy a night under the lights. Balance isn't a diet, it's a rhythm.

Aug 13-23Fringe Festival

Old Strathcona takes over for the Fringe. Our take: every show is about 45 minutes, and so is a class. Catch a show, then catch a class. You have time for both.

All monthDon't wait for September

Thinking of waiting until the fall to get going? Don't. The room is quiet, the coaches have more eyes for you, and starting now means you walk into September already moving instead of starting from zero with the crowd. StrongTEENS (11-16) and our Legends (50+) crew are both great places to start as school heads back.

Want a little extra support?

If you have questions about your training, your goals, or how to get back into it, we're here for you. Sometimes a quick chat is all it takes. Book a Goal Setting Session at blacktuskathletics.com, bring a friend to a class, or just reply and tell us what you want this fall to look like. We read every one.

See you in class,
Coach Jay and the Black Tusk team
